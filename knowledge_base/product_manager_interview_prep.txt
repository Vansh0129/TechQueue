# TechQueue Knowledge Base: Product Management Interview Guide

## 1. Product Sense & Design Framework (CIRCLES Method)
- Comprehend Situation: Clarify the goal, context, constraints, and platform.
- Identify Customer: Segment users and select the primary persona with the most acute pain point.
- Report Customer Needs: Map the user journey and highlight unmet needs.
- Cut Through Prioritization: Prioritize user needs based on impact, frequency, and feasibility.
- List Solutions: Brainstorm 3 distinct, creative solutions.
- Evaluate Trade-offs: Compare solutions against pros, cons, engineering complexity, and risks.
- Summarize Recommendation: Conclude with the chosen MVP, key metrics, and go-to-market strategy.

## 2. Prioritization Frameworks
- RICE: (Reach * Impact * Confidence) / Effort.
- MoSCoW: Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have.
- North Star Metric: The single metric that best captures the core value delivered to customers (e.g., Spotify: Time spent listening; Airbnb: Nights booked).
