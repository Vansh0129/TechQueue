# TechQueue Knowledge Base: HR & Culture-Fit Interview Guidelines

## 1. Common HR Screening Questions
- "Tell me about yourself":
  - Structure: Present (current role and recent wins) -> Past (relevant background and trajectory) -> Future (why this role and company align with your goals). Keep to 90-120 seconds.
- "Why do you want to work here?":
  - Connect your personal interests with the company's product, engineering challenges, or company culture. Mention specific tech stack components, open source contributions, or business milestones.
- "What are your greatest strengths and weaknesses?":
  - Strength: Back it with a concrete example and impact.
  - Weakness: Pick a genuine professional skill (not a fake weakness like "I work too hard"), and explain the proactive steps you are currently taking to improve.
- "Where do you see yourself in 3-5 years?":
  - Show ambition aligned with the engineering career ladder (e.g., Staff/Principal track or Engineering Management), mastering distributed systems, and driving larger architectural impact.

## 2. Navigating Compensation & Logistics
- Questions about salary expectations: Research market rates on Levels.fyi and Glassdoor for your level and location. Provide a realistic range or focus on total compensation (base, equity/bonus, benefits).
- Asking thoughtful questions to the interviewer:
  - "What does success look like in the first 90 days for this role?"
  - "How does the engineering team handle technical debt vs. feature velocity?"
  - "What is the most interesting technical challenge the team solved in the past quarter?"
